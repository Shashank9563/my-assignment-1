01.What is Data Science?
Data science is the field of study that combines domain expertise, programming skills, and math to extract meaningful insights from data.


02.What are the steps in the Data science loop?
The correct order of the steps from first to last is:
    .Question 
    .Data
    .Model
    .Decision
    .Chech-back


03.Dose Data Science always need AI
   NO.
   Reason: Many data science projects rely purely on traditional statistical analysis, basic data cleaning, and visual charts 
           to find answers without using any complex artificial intelligence or machine learning algorithms.
